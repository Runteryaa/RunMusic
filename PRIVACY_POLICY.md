# Privacy Policy for RunMusic

**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026  
**Application ID:** `com.runterya.music`

This Privacy Policy describes how **RunMusic** ("we", "our", or "the application") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

---

## 1. Overview & Data Collection

**RunMusic is an offline local audio player.**

- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, or physical location).
- **No Analytics / No Trackers:** RunMusic contains no third-party tracking SDKs, telemetry, advertising networks, or analytics tools.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account or sign in to use any feature of the application.

---

## 2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

RunMusic requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function as an offline music player on Android:

1. **Audio & Storage Access (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**:
   - **Purpose:** Used strictly to scan, read, and play local audio files (MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, etc.) stored on your device.
   - **Privacy:** Your audio files are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded, shared, or sent to any server.

2. **Image Access (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`)**:
   - **Purpose:** Used only if you choose to select a custom image from your gallery as album artwork for a track.
   - **Privacy:** Your photos remain on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted.

3. **Background Playback (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`, `WAKE_LOCK`)**:
   - **Purpose:** Required by the Android operating system to continue playing music when the app is minimized, when using other apps, or when the device screen is turned off, and to show playback controls in the notification bar.

---

## 3. Online Features (Lyrics & Cover Art)

- When searching for lyrics (via LRCLIB) or fetching public album metadata, RunMusic sends only public search queries (track title and artist name).
- No IP addresses, device identifiers, personal information, or user files are included in these search queries.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

RunMusic does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, nor does it collect any personal information from children or any other users.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

---

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository:  
https://github.com/Runteryaa/RunMusic
